# NovaStore Shipping and Delivery Policy

Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days and express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days. Orders over 75 dollars qualify for free standard shipping. Tracking numbers are emailed once the carrier scans the parcel. If a parcel has not moved for 5 business days, contact support and we will open a carrier investigation. Delivery addresses can be changed only before the order enters processing.
