# NovaStore Payments and Refund Timing

Refunds are sent to the original payment method after a return passes inspection and a staff member approves the refund. Card refunds typically appear within 5 to 10 business days. Gift card balances are restored immediately after approval. Refunds above 200 dollars always require a second approval from a senior staff member. Payment details are never collected in chat; staff will not ask for card numbers.
